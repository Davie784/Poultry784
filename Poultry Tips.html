Introduction to Poultry Farming

Poultry farming involves keeping birds such as chickens, ducks and turkeys for eggs, meat and income.

Good feeding, proper housing and disease prevention help farmers improve production.

1. Feeding and Nutrition

  1. Provide appropriate poultry feed.
  2. Provide clean drinking water every day.
  3. Maintain regular feeding schedules.
  4. Choose feed suitable for the age and type of bird.

Remember: Clean water and balanced feed are important for healthy poultry growth.

2. Housing and Hygiene

  • Keep poultry houses clean and dry.
  • Provide adequate ventilation.
  • Avoid overcrowding.
  • Protect birds from predators and harsh weather.

3. Disease Prevention

  • Maintain cleanliness in poultry houses.
  • Monitor birds regularly for signs of illness.
  • Follow recommended vaccination programmes.
  • Seek professional veterinary advice when necessary.

Farmers should contact a qualified veterinary professional when birds show unusual signs of illness.

4. Marketing and Record Keeping

Farmers should record poultry production, expenses, sales and other important farm activities.

Keeping records helps farmers calculate costs, monitor profits and make better business decisions.
